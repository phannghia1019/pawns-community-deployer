# Peer Bandwidth Sharing Agreement & Consent Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy governs the deployment of the official Pawns.app client binary/container across participant-owned computers, home lab servers, and personal networking equipment.

## 2. Participant Rights & Safeguards
- **Explicit Prior Consent**: No device shall run Pawns CLI without documented consent from the primary hardware owner.
- **Resource Limitation**: Bandwidth sharing is restricted to unused uplink/downlink headroom.
- **Data Privacy Guarantee**: The software operates strictly as a network relay proxy. It has zero capability or permission to inspect, log, access, or modify local files, storage, personal communications, or private network traffic.
- **Immediate Termination**: Any participant may stop sharing or uninstall the software at any time without penalty.
